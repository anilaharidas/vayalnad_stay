Our Story

A farmhouse that grew into a homestay

We opened our doors to guests because the valley felt like something worth sharing — not because we set out to run a hotel.

The family

Hosted by the family who lives here

The family behind Vayalnad Stay has lived in this valley for years. The guest rooms were added onto the family home so visitors could share the same quiet setting — and the same table.

Someone from the family is always close by to help: planning day trips, pointing you to a good local meal, or simply leaving you to a book on the veranda.

What we care about

How we like to host

Light on the land

We try to tread lightly on the valley — mindful of water and power, growing what we can, and keeping waste down.

Local first

The people who guide, drive and cook for our guests are all from the area, so a stay here supports the wider valley.

Your pace

Some guests want every day planned; others want a hammock and nothing else. Tell us which and we'll shape the stay around it.

The land

The grounds

The grounds run from the house down to a quiet stretch of water, with trees for shade and open sky at night. You're free to wander.

  • Garden and shade trees
  • Water along the lower boundary
  • Space to walk and sit
  • Dark skies at night
